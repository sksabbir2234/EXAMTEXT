১.১ — Parts of Speech & Verbs

✦ Entry 01 — Verb (Action Word)

Verb হলো সেই শব্দ যা কোনো action, occurrence বা state of being প্রকাশ করে। এটি subject কী করে, কী ঘটে বা কী তা বোঝায়। প্রতিটি সম্পূর্ণ sentence-এ অন্তত একটি verb থাকতে হবে। Verb তিন ধরনের: physical action (run, write, eat), mental action (think, believe, consider), এবং state of being (be, exist, seem)। গঠন: Subject + Verb + (Object/Complement)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Verb = action বা state প্রকাশক শব্দ
  • প্রতিটি বাক্যে অন্তত একটি verb প্রয়োজন
  • Verb physical, mental বা state — তিন ধরনের
  • গঠন: Subject + Verb + (Object/Complement)
  • Verb আর noun-এর পার্থক্য বুঝতে হবে

✦ Entry 02 — Word Class in Context (dress)

ইংরেজিতে অনেক শব্দ একাধিক part of speech হিসেবে কাজ করতে পারে। শব্দটির context বা বাক্যে তার অবস্থান নির্ধারণ করে এটি noun, verb, adjective নাকি অন্য কিছু। "You should dress for cold weather today" — এখানে 'dress' modal auxiliary 'should'-এর পরে বসেছে। Modal auxiliaries (can, could, shall, should, will, would, may, might, must) সবসময় base form of verb নেয়।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • একই শব্দ বিভিন্ন part of speech হতে পারে
  • Context-ই শব্দের class নির্ধারণ করে
  • Modal auxiliary-এর পর সবসময় base verb বসে
  • "dress" noun (পোশাক) বা verb (পরা) — দুটোই
  • বাক্যে শব্দের position দেখে class চিনতে হবে

✦ Entry 03 — Principal vs Auxiliary Verb (did)

Principal verb বা main verb বাক্যের মূল অর্থ বহন করে এবং একা দাঁড়াতে পারে। Auxiliary verb মূল verb-কে tense, mood বা voice গঠনে সাহায্য করে। "I did the work" — এখানে 'did' একাই আছে, অন্য কোনো verb নেই, তাই এটি principal verb। "I did not go" — এখানে 'did' auxiliary ('go'-কে সাহায্য করছে) এবং 'go' principal verb।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Principal verb = মূল অর্থ বহনকারী verb (একা থাকতে পারে)
  • Auxiliary verb = সাহায্যকারী verb
  • Do/Be/Have প্রধান বা auxiliary — দুটোই হতে পারে
  • 'Did' একা থাকলে principal, অন্য verb-এর সাথে থাকলে auxiliary

✦ Entry 04 — Intransitive Verb (sleep)

Intransitive verb এমন একটি verb যা direct object নেয় না। Action subject-এ শুরু হয় এবং subject-এ শেষ হয় — এটি কোনো কিছুতে transfer হয় না। "She sleeps" — 'sleeps' কী? বা 'sleeps' কাকে? — এই প্রশ্নের উত্তর নেই। সাধারণ intransitive verb: sleep, arrive, come, go, sit, stand, die, fall, laugh, cry।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Intransitive verb-এর direct object লাগে না
  • Action subject-এর মধ্যেই সীমাবদ্ধ থাকে
  • 'কী?' বা 'কাকে?' — প্রশ্নের উত্তর পাওয়া যায় না
  • উদাহরণ: sleep, sit, stand, arrive, die
  • Verb-এর পরের word object না-ও হতে পারে (যেমন adverb)

✦ Entry 05 — Transitive → Intransitive (Quasi-Passive)

কিছু transitive verb কে intransitive অর্থে ব্যবহার করা যায়, যেখানে original object টি subject হয় এবং verb-টি active form-এ থাকলেও অর্থ passive হয়। একে quasi-passive বা active-passive voice বলে। "She milks the cow" → "The cow is milking" (গাভীটি দোহানো যাচ্ছে)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Quasi-passive-এ active form-এ passive অর্থ প্রকাশ পায়
  • Original object নতুন subject হয়
  • Verb sell, read, write, cook, wash, milk, peel — quasi-passive হতে পারে
  • "The cow is milking" = active form, passive meaning
  • Subject-টি গুণগতভাবে action গ্রহণের ক্ষমতা রাখে

✦ Entry 06 — Linking Verb (went)

Linking verb subject-কে subject complement-এর সাথে সংযুক্ত করে। এটি action প্রকাশ করে না; বরং subject আর complement-এর মাঝে একটি সমান চিহ্নের (=) মতো কাজ করে। "He went mad" — এখানে 'went' শারীরিক movement নয়, বরং subject 'He'-কে adjective 'mad'-এর সাথে linking করছে। 'Go' linking verb হয় যখন এটি become অর্থে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Linking verb = subject আর complement-এর সংযোগকারী
  • It acts like an = sign between subject and complement
  • Go + adjective = linking (become অর্থে)
  • Common linking verbs: be, become, seem, appear, look, feel
  • Action verb নয় — state বা condition বোঝায়

✦ Entry 07 — Causative Verb (raised)

Causative verb প্রকাশ করে যে subject নিজে action না করে অন্য কাউকে বা কিছুকে দিয়ে action করায়। "rise" (intransitive — নিজে উপরে ওঠা) vs "raise" (causative — অন্যকে উপরে তোলানো)। "He raised his hand" — তিনি নিজের হাত নিজে উপরে তুললেন। Causative verb pairs: rise/raise, lie/lay, sit/set।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Causative verb = subject action নিজে না করে করায়
  • Rise (নিজে ওঠা) vs Raise (তুলে দেওয়া)
  • Lie (শোওয়া) vs Lay (রাখা)
  • Sit (বসা) vs Set (বসানো/স্থাপন করা)
  • প্রথমটি intransitive, দ্বিতীয়টি causative/transitive

✦ Entry 08 — Causative Have + Person + Bare Infinitive

Causative 'have' ব্যবহার করা হয় subject যখন অন্য কাউকে দিয়ে কিছু করায়। গঠন: have/has/had + person + base verb (bare infinitive — "to" ছাড়া)। "Rafiq had Mizan clean the floor" — রফিক নিজে পরিষ্কার করেনি, মিজানকে দিয়ে করিয়েছে। Verb-টি base form-এ থাকবে, 'to' যুক্ত হবে না।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Have + person + base verb (bare infinitive)
  • 'To' ব্যবহার করা যাবে না
  • Subject নিজে না করে অন্যকে দিয়ে করায়
  • Things-এর সাথে past participle: "I had my car repaired"
  • Have (causative) ≠ Have (possessive)

✦ Entry 09 — Causative Make + Object + Bare Infinitive

Causative 'make' মানে 'কাউকে কিছু করতে বাধ্য করা' বা 'করে ফেলা'। গঠন: make + object + base verb (bare infinitive)। Active voice-এ 'make'-এর পরে 'to' ব্যবহার করা যায় না। "The speaker failed to make the audience listen to him patiently" — speaker audience-কে শোনার জন্য বাধ্য করতে চেয়েছিল। Passive-এ 'to' ফিরে আসে: "The audience was made to listen."

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Make + object + base verb (active)
  • Active-এ 'to' ব্যবহৃত হয় না
  • Passive-এ 'to' ফিরে আসে: "was made to + V1"
  • Make = বাধ্য করা, force/compel অর্থে
  • Have (cause) vs Make (force) — পার্থক্য বুঝতে হবে

১.২ — Subject-Verb Agreement

✦ Entry 10 — S-V Agreement — Scope

Subject-Verb Agreement হল grammatical rule যেখানে verb-কে তার subject-এর সাথে number (singular/plural) এবং person (1st/2nd/3rd)-এ match করতে হয়। Gender ইংরেজিতে verb form-কে প্রভাবিত করে না। Singular subject → singular verb; plural subject → plural verb।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Verb matches subject in Number & Person
  • Gender does NOT affect verb form in English
  • Singular subject → verb + s/es
  • Plural subject → base verb
  • 3rd person singular-এ -s/-es যোগ হয়

✦ Entry 11 — Letter Name as Subject (I)

যখন কোনো letter, word বা quoted term বাক্যের subject হয়, তখন এটি third person singular noun হিসেবে গণ্য হয় — এমনকি শব্দটি 'I' হলেও। "I is the 9th letter of the English alphabet" — এখানে 'I' অক্ষরটির নাম হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত হয়েছে, pronoun হিসেবে নয়। তাই 'is' ব্যবহার হয়েছে।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Letter/word as subject = 3rd person singular
  • "I" অক্ষরটির নাম হিসেবে 'is' নেয়, 'am' নয়
  • "Go is an irregular verb" — word হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত
  • "They is a plural pronoun" — শব্দটির নাম হিসেবে
  • Context দেখে বুঝতে হবে pronoun না word name

✦ Entry 82 — Active vs Passive Voice

Active voice-এ subject action করে: "The cat ate the fish" (cat = subject, ate = action, fish = object)। Passive voice-এ object subject হয়ে যায় এবং action সেই subject-এর ওপর ঘটে: "The fish was eaten by the cat"। Passive গঠন: be + V3। Tense অনুযায়ী 'be' পরিবর্তিত হয় (is/am/are/was/were/been/being)।

  • Agent (by + subject) optional

✦ Entry 95 — Relative Clause — Defining vs Non-defining

Defining Relative Clause (no commas) noun-কে নির্দিষ্ট করে — ছাড়া বাক্যের অর্থ অসম্পূর্ণ। "The man who lives next door is a doctor" — কোন ম্যান? নির্দিষ্ট করে বলছে। Non-defining Relative Clause (commas সহ) শুধু অতিরিক্ত তথ্য — ছাড়লেও মূল অর্থ অপরিবর্তিত। "My father, who is a doctor, lives in Dhaka" — 'father' already specific।

  • Non-defining-এ 'that' ব্যবহার করা যায় না

✦ Entry 96 — Modal Perfect (Must have, Could have, etc.)

১.৪ — Articles (A, An, The)

✦ Entry 114 — When to Use A / An

A ব্যবহৃত হয় consonant sound-এর আগে এবং An ব্যবহৃত হয় vowel sound-এর আগে — অক্ষর নয়, উচ্চারণ দেখে। 'a cat' কিন্তু 'an apple'। বিশেষ ক্ষেত্রে 'u' যদি 'yu' শোনায় (university, European, one) তবে 'a' হয়; 'h' যদি silent হয় (hour, honest) তবে 'an' হয়।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • a + consonant sound, an + vowel sound
  • a university / a one-eyed man (উচ্চারণ 'y')
  • an hour / an honest ('h' silent)
  • singular countable noun-এ সাধারণ উল্লেখে

✦ Entry 115 — When to Use The

The হলো definite article — নির্দিষ্ট/জানা বস্তুর ক্ষেত্রে। বিশেষভাবে বসে: superlative (the best), unique noun (the sun, the moon), নদী/পর্বত/মহাসাগর (the Padma, the Himalayas, the Pacific), ordinal number (the first), musical instrument (the guitar)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • The = নির্দিষ্ট/definite
  • superlative-এর আগে: the best
  • unique noun: the sun, the moon
  • নদী/পর্বত/মহাসাগর: the Padma

✦ Entry 116 — When to Use NO Article (Zero Article)

অনেক ক্ষেত্রে কোনো article-ই লাগে না। Zero article ব্যবহৃত হয়: ভাষা (Bengali, English), খাবার/meal (breakfast, lunch), খেলা/game (cricket, football), proper noun (Dhaka, Rina, India), abstract noun সাধারণ অর্থে (Love is great)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • ভাষা: Bengali, English → no article
  • meal: breakfast, dinner → no article
  • game/sport: cricket, chess → no article
  • proper noun: Dhaka, Karim → no article

✦ Entry 117 — The + Adjective = Plural Noun

The + adjective দরিদ্র/বিশেষ শ্রেণির লোককে বোঝায় এবং এটি plural noun হিসেবে কাজ করে: the rich = ধনীরা, the poor = গরিবরা, the blind, the deaf, the dead। এরা সবসময় plural verb নেয়: 'The rich are happy' (NOT 'is')।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • the rich / the poor = plural noun
  • the blind, the deaf, the dead
  • সবসময় plural verb ('are', 'were')
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'The poor is suffering' (ভুল)। 'the + adjective' plural তাই 'The poor are suffering' হবে।

✦ Entry 118 — Article with Proper Nouns

১.৫ — Determiners (Some, Any, All, Every)

✦ Entry 119 — Determiners (Basic)

Determiners হলো যে শব্দগুলো noun-এর আগে বসে এবং নির্দিষ্টতা বা পরিমাণ নির্ধারণ করে। প্রধান determiners: some (affirmative), any (negative/interrogative), no (negation), every (singular), each (singular), all (plural)। 'Some' ব্যবহার হয় offers/requests-তেও: 'Would you like some tea?'

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • some + plural/uncountable (affirmative)
  • any + plural/uncountable (negative/interrogative)
  • no + singular/plural (negation)
  • every + singular noun
  • some সুপারিশ/অনুরোধেও: 'Give me some water'

✦ Entry 120 — Quantifiers: Much, Many, Few, Little

Much/Little = uncountable noun-এ; Many/Few = plural countable noun-এ। 'A few' = কিছু (positive), 'few' = প্রায় নেই (negative tone)। 'A little' = কিছু (positive), 'little' = প্রায় নেই (negative tone)। 'Less' = uncountable-এ, 'fewer' = countable-এ।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • much / a little + uncountable
  • many / a few + plural countable
  • few / little = negative tone (প্রায় নেই)
  • a few / a little = positive tone (কিছু আছে)
  • less + uncountable, fewer + countable

✦ Entry 121 — Partitive Constructions (A Piece of, A Cup of)

Partitive constructions ব্যবহার করে uncountable noun-কে countable করা হয়। 'a piece of paper', 'a cup of tea', 'a loaf of bread', 'a piece of furniture', 'a slice of cake'। 'Furniture', 'advice', 'information', 'equipment', 'luggage', 'homework' — এরা সবসময় uncountable, 'furnitures'/'advices' গ্রামার ভুল।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • a piece of paper / advice / furniture
  • a cup of tea / coffee
  • a loaf of bread
  • furniture, advice, information → always uncountable
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'Give me an advice' (ভুল)। 'advice' uncountable → 'Give me some advice' বা 'a piece of advice'।

১.৬ — Prepositions (In, On, At, For, With)

✦ Entry 122 — Time Prepositions (In, On, At)

In = মাস/বছর/মৌসুম (in January, in 2024, in summer)। On = নির্দিষ্ট তারিখ/দিন (on Monday, on 1st May)। At = নির্দিষ্ট সময় (at 5 pm, at noon, at night, at dawn)। 'On Monday morning' কিন্তু 'in the morning'। 'At the weekend' (BrE) বা 'on the weekend' (AmE)।

  • at + time: at 3 pm, at noon, at night
  • on Monday morning (দিন + সময়)

✦ Entry 123 — Place Prepositions (In, On, At, To)

সারসংক্ষেপ:

১.৭ — Narration (Reported Speech)

✦ Entry 125 — Tense Shift in Indirect Speech

Direct speech → Indirect speech-এ reporting verb past tense হলে reported speech-এর tense বদলায়। Simple present → simple past, present continuous → past continuous, present perfect → past perfect, will → would, can → could, may → might। 'said to' → 'told'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • S → P → PP, PP → P.Past, will → would
  • can → could, may → might, shall → should
  • said to → told
  • 'that' conjunction-এর সাথে indirect speech

✦ Entry 126 — Pronoun & Time/Place Changes

Indirect speech-এ pronoun বদলায়: I → he/she, we → they, my → his/her। Time words বদলায়: today → that day, yesterday → the previous day/the day before, tomorrow → the next day/the following day, last year → the previous year, ago → before। Place: here → there, this → that, these → those।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • I → he/she, my → his/her, we → they
  • today → that day, yesterday → the previous day
  • tomorrow → the next day, here → there
  • this → that, these → those, ago → before

✦ Entry 127 — Imperative Sentences

Imperative sentence-এ to + verb (infinitive) ব্যবহার হয়: He said, "Go there." → He told me to go there। Negative: He said, "Don't go." → He told me not to go। 'Ordered', 'requested', 'advised', 'suggested' — reporting verb হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Command/request → to + verb
  • Negative → not to + verb
  • told/ordered/requested/advised + object + to + verb
  • 'Let me go' → requested to let him go
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'He told me that go there' (ভুল)। Imperative-এ 'that' নয়, 'to + verb' হয়।

✦ Entry 128 — Interrogative Sentences (Yes/No & Wh-Questions)

Yes/No question-এ 'if' বা 'whether' ব্যবহার হয়: He asked me if I was coming। Wh-question-এ 'that' নয়, শুধু question word রাখা হয়: He asked where I lived। 'Did' auxiliary-সহ present tense → past tense হয়: 'Do you like it?' → He asked me if I liked it।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Yes/No → if / whether
  • Wh-question → wh-word + statement order
  • Do you...? → asked if I... (tense shift)
  • Reporting verb: asked, inquired, wanted to know

✦ Entry 129 — Exclamatory Sentences

Exclamatory sentence-এ 'exclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise' ব্যবহৃত হয়: He said, "What a beautiful flower!" → He exclaimed with joy that it was a very beautiful flower। 'How' → 'that...was/were very': 'How nice!' → exclaimed that it was very nice। 'What a/an' → 'that it was a/an'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • What a/an + noun → exclaimed + that it was
  • How + adjective → exclaimed + that it was very
  • exclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise
  • interjection (!) → that clause

১.৮ — Voice (Active & Passive)

✦ Entry 130 — Active Voice to Passive Voice (Basic)

Active voice: Subject + Verb + Object। Passive voice: Object (as subject) + be + past participle + by + agent। Passive-এ object subject হয় এবং verb-এ 'to be' + V3 (past participle) বসে। 'The boy kicked the ball' → 'The ball was kicked by the boy।'

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Object → Subject, be + V3 + by + agent
  • Active: S + V + O → Passive: O + be + V3 + by S
  • 'by' agent optional যদি agent unknown
  • Tense 'to be' verb-এ প্রতিফলিত হয়

✦ Entry 131 — Passive Voice: Tense-by-Tense Transformation

প্রতিটি tense-এ passive form আলাদাভাবে তৈরি হয়। Present simple: am/is/are + V3। Past simple: was/were + V3। Future simple: will be + V3। Present continuous: am/is/are being + V3। Present perfect: have/has been + V3। Modal: modal + be + V3।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Present simple: is/am/are + V3
  • Past simple: was/were + V3
  • Future: will be + V3
  • Present continuous: is being + V3
  • Present perfect: has been + V3
  • Modal: can be + V3, should be + V3

✦ Entry 132 — Special Passive Constructions

কিছু special constructions: 'It is said that' (passive impersonal), 'People believe that' → 'It is believed that', 'They say that' → 'It is said that'। Modal passive: can be done, must be done, should be done। 'Have to' → 'has to be done'। Infinitive passive: to be done। Gerund passive: being done।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • People say → It is said
  • can be done, must be done, should be done
  • Infinitive: to be + V3
  • Gerund: being + V3
  • Impersonal passive: It is believed that...
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'The work is doing' (ভুল)। Passive-এ 'be' + V3 লাগে: 'The work is being done'।

✦ Entry 133 — Passive with Two Objects

যদি verb-এর দুটি object থাকে (ditransitive verb), দুভাবে passive করা যায়। 'He gave me a book' → 'I was given a book (by him)' অথবা 'A book was given to me (by him)'। 'send/offer/show/teach/lend/pay' — এরা সব ditransitive। 'To' preposition বসে যদি indirect object passive subject হয়।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • He gave me a book → I was given a book
  • A book was given to me (by him)
  • ditransitive: give, send, show, teach, lend, pay
  • Indirect object → subject: no 'to'
  • Direct object → subject: 'to' যোগ হয়

১.৯ — Conditionals (If Clauses)

✦ Entry 134 — Zero Conditional

Zero conditional ব্যবহার হয় সর্বদা সত্য বা বৈজ্ঞানিক তথ্য বোঝাতে: If + present, present। 'If you heat water, it boils.' 'If it rains, the ground gets wet.' — এখানে time reference নেই, সবসময় true।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • If + present, present
  • বৈজ্ঞানিক তথ্য/সর্বদা সত্য
  • If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
  • If + present = present tense দুটোই

✦ Entry 135 — First Conditional

First conditional ব্যবহার হয় ভবিষ্যতের সম্ভাব্য ঘটনায়: If + present, will + verb। 'If it rains, I will stay home.' 'If you study hard, you will pass.' — condition present tense-এ, result future।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • If + present, will + base verb
  • ভবিষ্যতের সম্ভাব্য ঘটনা
  • If you help me, I will help you.
  • can/may/might বসতে পারে will-এর জায়গায়

✦ Entry 136 — Second Conditional

Second conditional ব্যবহার হয় অবাস্তব বা কল্পনাতমক পরিস্থিতিতে: If + past (subjunctive), would + verb। 'If I were rich, I would buy a car.' 'If I were you, I would accept the offer.' — 'were' সবসময় I/he/she তে (was নয়, formal grammar)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • If + past, would + base verb
  • অবাস্তব/কল্পনাতমক পরিস্থিতি
  • If I were you, I would go.
  • If I were rich, I would travel the world.
  • If + were (I/he/she) — formal subjunctive
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'If I was rich, I would buy a car' (informal acceptable but exam-এ 'were' লিখুন)। Formal: 'If I were rich'।

✦ Entry 137 — Third Conditional

Third conditional ব্যবহার হয় অতীতের অবাস্তব পরিস্থিতিতে: If + past perfect, would have + V3। 'If I had studied, I would have passed.' 'If it had rained, we would have stayed home.' — অতীত ঘটেনি, তাই 'would have'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • If + past perfect, would have + V3
  • অতীতের অবাস্তব/না-ঘটা পরিস্থিতি
  • If I had come earlier, I would have met you.
  • If + had + V3, would have + V3

১.১০ — Modals (Can, Could, May, Might, Should, Must)

✦ Entry 138 — Can, Could, Be Able To

Can = সক্ষমতা (present), অনুমতি। Could = past ability, সম্ভাবনা, formal request। Be able to = any tense-এ ability বোঝাতে ব্যবহৃত হয় (will be able to, have been able to)। 'Can' past tense-এ 'could', perfect-এ 'have been able to'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • can + base verb (present ability/permission)
  • could + base verb (past ability/possibility)
  • be able to (any tense)
  • could = polite request: Could you help?
  • can have + V3 (past ability, limited use)

✦ Entry 139 — May, Might, Be Allowed To

May = অনুমতি (formal), সম্ভাবনা (maybe 50%)। Might = কম সম্ভাবনা (less than may), past permission। 'May I come in?' (permission)। 'He may come' (possibility)। 'Might' more uncertain than 'may'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • may + base verb (permission/possibility)
  • might + base verb (less possibility)
  • May I...? (formal permission)
  • might past tense-এ permission: he said I might go
  • may have + V3 (past possibility): He may have left

✦ Entry 140 — Should, Ought To, Had Better

Should = উচিত (advice/duty), expectation। Ought to = should-এর মতোই কিন্তু slightly stronger (moral duty)। Had better = উচিত (urgent advice, warning), contraction: 'd better। 'You should study' = 'You ought to study'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • should + base verb (advice/duty)
  • ought to + base verb (moral duty, stronger)
  • had better + base verb (urgent/warning)
  • should have + V3 (past regret): I should have studied
  • ought not to (negative)
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'You had better to go' (ভুল)। Had better + base verb (without 'to'): 'You had better go'।

✦ Entry 141 — Must, Have To, Need

Must = আবশ্যক (strong obligation/personal), deduction। Have to = আবশ্যক (external authority)। 'Must' present + future; 'had to' past। 'You must do it' (I say so) vs 'You have to do it' (rule says so)। 'Need not' = 'don't need to' (no obligation, negative)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • must + base verb (strong obligation/deduction)
  • have to + base verb (external obligation)
  • must not = নিষেধ (prohibition)
  • need not = don't need to (no obligation)
  • must have + V3 (past deduction): He must have gone
  • had to = past of have to

  1. can + base verb (ability/permission)।
  2. could + base verb (past ability/polite request)।
  3. be able to (any tense)।
  4. may + base verb (permission/possibility)।
  5. might + base verb (less possibility)।
  6. should + base verb (advice/duty)।
  7. ought to + base verb (moral duty)।
  8. had better + base verb (urgent advice)।
  9. must + base verb (strong obligation)।
  10. have to + base verb (external obligation)।
  11. must not = prohibition; need not = no obligation।
  12. should have + V3 (past regret): I should have studied।
  13. must have + V3 (past deduction): He must have left।
  14. need not have + V3 (unnecessary action): You need not have bought it।

১.১১ — Pronouns

✦ Entry 142 — Personal & Reflexive Pronouns

Personal pronouns: I/me, he/him, she/her, we/us, they/them, you, it। Reflexive pronouns: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves — subject-এর সাথে একই, object-এ ব্যবহৃত হয়: 'He hurt himself.' 'I saw myself in the mirror.'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • I/he/she/we/they → subject; me/him/her/us/them → object
  • Reflexive: -self (singular), -selves (plural)
  • He hurt himself (not 'hisself')
  • myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself

✦ Entry 143 — Possessive & Demonstrative Pronouns

Possessive: my/mine, his, her/hers, our/ours, their/theirs, your/yours। 'Mine' = 'my + noun': 'This book is mine.' Demonstrative: this/these (near), that/those (far). 'This' singular, 'these' plural। 'Those who work hard succeed.'

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • my/mine, his, her/hers, our/ours, their/theirs
  • mine = my + noun: This is mine.
  • this/these = near; that/those = far
  • this/these + singular; that/those + plural

✦ Entry 144 — Relative Pronouns (Who, Whom, Which, That, Whose)

Who = person subject: 'The man who came.' Whom = person object: 'The man whom I met.' Which = thing: 'The book which I read.' That = person/thing (restrictive): 'The house that I bought.' Whose = possession: 'The boy whose father is a doctor.'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • who = person (subject)
  • whom = person (object)
  • which = thing
  • that = person/thing (restrictive only)
  • whose = possession

✦ Entry 145 — Indefinite & Interrogative Pronouns

Indefinite: someone, anybody, everything, nothing, each, either, neither, all, both, none। Interrogative: who, whom, whose, which, what। 'Who' = subject, 'whom' = object। 'What do you want?' 'Whose is this?' 'Which do you prefer?'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • someone, anyone, everyone, no one
  • something, anything, everything, nothing
  • each/either/neither + singular verb
  • who (subject), whom (object), whose (possession)
  • all/both/none + plural (usually)
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'Everyone have their own opinion' (ভুল)। 'everyone' singular → 'Everyone has his/her own opinion'।

১.১৫ — Sentence Structure

✦ Entry 149 — Basic Sentence Patterns

ইংরেজিতে ৫টি মৌলিক sentence pattern আছে: S+V (He sleeps), S+V+O (She reads a book), S+V+C (He is a doctor), S+V+IO+DO (She gave me a pen), S+V+O+C (They made him captain)। 'S' = Subject, 'V' = Verb, 'O' = Object, 'C' = Complement, 'IO' = Indirect Object, 'DO' = Direct Object।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • S+V: Intransitive (He sleeps)
  • S+V+O: Transitive (She reads a book)
  • S+V+C: Linking verb (He is a doctor)
  • S+V+IO+DO: Ditransitive (She gave me a pen)
  • S+V+O+C: Complex transitive (They made him captain)

✦ Entry 150 — Sentence Types (Declarative, Interrogative, Imperative, Exclamatory)

Declarative: statement (He is a student.)। Interrogative: question (Is he a student?)। Imperative: command/request (Go there. / Please sit down.)। Exclamatory: strong feeling (What a beautiful flower!)। Affirmative: 'He goes.' Negative: 'He does not go.'।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Declarative: statement ending with period
  • Interrogative: question ending with question mark
  • Imperative: command/request (no subject)
  • Exclamatory: strong feeling (exclamation mark)
  • Affirmative vs Negative

✦ Entry 151 — Subject-Verb Agreement (Advanced)

Subject-verb agreement নিয়ম: compound subject + 'and' → plural verb ('Tom and Jerry are friends')। 'Either...or' / 'neither...nor' → verb agrees with nearest subject ('Either he or I am wrong')। Collective noun → singular in BrE ('The team is playing')।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Singular subject → singular verb
  • Plural subject → plural verb
  • Either...or → verb with nearest subject
  • Neither...nor → verb with nearest subject
  • Collective noun: team, group, family → singular (BrE)
  • Money, news → singular (uncountable)
⚠️ Common Mistake: 'The news are good' (ভুল)। 'news' uncountable → 'The news is good'।

✦ Entry 152 — Common Errors in Sentence Construction

পরীক্ষায় সবচেয়ে বেশি ভুল হয়: dangling modifiers ('Walking to school, the rain started' — ভুল), parallel structure ('I like reading, writing, and to swim' — ভুল, 'to swim' নয়, 'swimming' হবে), comma splice ('I went home, she stayed' — ভুল, 'I went home; she stayed' হবে)।

সারসংক্ষেপ:
  • Dangling modifier: subject must follow the modifier
  • Parallel structure: same form in lists
  • Comma splice: use semicolon or conjunction
  • Misplaced modifier: keep modifier next to word it modifies
  • Run-on sentence: two sentences without conjunction/punctuation
