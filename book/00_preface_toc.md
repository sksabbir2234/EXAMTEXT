## How to Use This Book

1. **Each entry is self-contained**: Read the Concept, try the Q, check the A, memorize the tip.
2. **Study in layers**: First read all Concept explanations, then attempt all questions, then review mistakes.
3. **Use the appendix tables**: For last-minute revision, the appendix tables condense all facts into compact references.
4. **Take the chapter quizzes**: After finishing a chapter, attempt the MCQs without looking back.
